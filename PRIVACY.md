# Privacy

OmniShade is a local, per-game post-processing runtime. It does not operate an OmniVex gameplay-telemetry, advertising, account, or usage-analytics service.

## Local data

Installation state, backups, configuration, presets, logs, and recovery information stay with the selected game or on the local computer. OmniShade does not require users to upload gameplay data.

## Network and third-party boundaries

Games, launchers, drivers, anti-cheat systems, GitHub, Patreon, Ko-fi, and email are independent services with their own behavior and privacy terms. Their traffic is not OmniShade telemetry.

## Support

Only share privacy-redacted diagnostic material. Remove usernames, game-library paths, account identifiers, license information, and unrelated process data before opening an issue or sending email.
